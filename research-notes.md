## Source-quality caveats
- **Primary strength:** Stanford Medicine news desk + AAAS/EurekAlert press package + detailed bioRxiv full text with methods, stats, and limitations. Science publication date is reported by Stanford/EurekAlert as Sept 17, 2026.
- **Naming nuance:** Stanford news says a “private, well-established pharmaceutical company” independently arrived at the B7-H3 ADC strategy; the bioRxiv text identifies **ifinatamab deruxtecan** and breakthrough designation. Blog uses the named drug where the preprint does, and notes independent convergence carefully (not claiming the Virtual Biotech invented B7-H3 research wholesale).
- **Agent count wording:** Stanford headline/news uses “37,000 employees”; paper focuses on **37,075 clinical-trialist agents** for trial curation within a larger multi-agent org (11 core scientist/office agents + swarm). Blog uses both framings accurately.
- **Trial count:** Prefer precise **55,984** from paper/EurekAlert over Stanford’s rounded “some 50,000.”
- **Observational stats:** Success associations are statistical associations from curated trial data, not randomized proof that picking switch-like targets *causes* approval. Blog flags this.
- **AllSci** is a secondary brief; not used as sole evidence for any number.
- No fabricated quotes, studies, or percentages; all quantitative claims trace to the sources above.
