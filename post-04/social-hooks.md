# Social hooks — post-04

## 5 share hooks

1. **Hook (curiosity gap):** A ringing bell seems to fade smoothly. Quantum mechanics says the fade is a staircase. Stanford just watched the last step — one phonon to zero — in real time.

2. **Hook (counterintuitive science):** Sound can quantum-jump. Photons did it in 2007. Trapped ions in 1986. Mechanical phonons just joined the club (*Science*, Sep 17, 2026).

3. **Hook (stat punch):** 2.1 ms of ringdown. 294 qubit checks per trajectory. 8,447 open trajectories on Zenodo. 85% single-phonon heralding. Then an abrupt flip to silence.

4. **Hook (tech stakes):** In quantum hardware, a jump is often an error. You can’t correct what you can’t see. Catching a phonon disappear is a diagnostic milestone — not a finished quantum computer.

5. **Hook (identity / shareability):** Your ear hears a smooth fade. At the quantum floor, vibration ends with a step. Scientists finally caught sound mid-jump.
