## Caveats for editors / Website Developer

1. **No photographed phonon / no audible snap:** Always frame as inferred from qubit measurement records. Do not claim a camera or microphone caught the jump.
2. **Not a quantum computer demo:** Error *detection* foundation, not demonstrated error correction. Do not overclaim product readiness or smartphone quantum upgrades (Szakiel’s quote is aspirational, long-horizon).
3. **Back-action:** Continuous checks shortened lifetime (~2.1 ms → ~649 µs). Mentioning this protects credibility.
4. **Prior art boundary:** Single phonons have been created/detected before; ions (1986) and photons (2007) already showed quantum jumps. The novelty is *real-time individual jump trajectories of mechanical sound*.
5. **Author order:** Makihara & Szakiel are co-first; Safavi-Naeini leads / corresponding. David Schuster and SLAC’s Shannon Harvey are coauthors — don’t omit if expanding credits.
6. **Funding / Amazon Scholars:** Safavi-Naeini and Schuster are Amazon Scholars; AWS among funders. Disclose if editors want conflict transparency; not required for a science explainer but good practice.
7. **Publication date:** Institutional drop is Sep 17, 2026; ScienceBlog amplification Sep 20. Do not write “discovered today” as if Sep 20 were the VoR date unless confirming *Science* online date independently.
8. **Quotes:** Only use Safavi-Naeini / Makihara / Szakiel lines from Stanford/EurekAlert — do not fabricate.
