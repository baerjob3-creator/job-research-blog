## Caveats for editors / Website Developer

1. **Not a “thinking mouse” / not consciousness claims:** Never imply human minds in mice. Prefer “research platform for neurodevelopment, disease, and therapeutics.”
2. **Anatomical ≠ functional necessity:** Spinal projections and host inputs are connectivity evidence; authors caution against claiming pathway-specific behavioral sufficiency.
3. **VEN-like, not proven mature VENs:** Morphology-based identification; do not overclaim full molecular identity.
4. **Differentiate from post-02 (BCI) and post-16 (BlueRock):** Organoid developmental transplant ≠ neural implant decoding ≠ clinical Parkinson’s DA graft.
5. **Grafts are immature:** Incomplete layering/arealization, limited GABAergic interneurons, immature network dynamics, human–mouse developmental tempo mismatch — quote these when space allows.
6. **Y-maze nuance:** XCX above chance vs apallial not ≠ restored human working memory.
7. **Hypoxia is a CP-risk model, not an FTD cure:** Demonstrated injury readout is perinatal hypoxia; FTD/VEN angle is prospective platform use.
8. **Ethics mandatory in shares:** Authors consulted bioethicists / independent committee; Asilomar 2025 context exists — do not skip in longform.
9. **IP disclosure:** Stanford OTL patents/provisional on organoids/transplant; Pașca inventor — fine to note briefly, not to sensationalize.
10. **Transplant age phrasing:** Stanford news mentions ~2-day-old pups; News-Medical/paper summaries cite 5–17 days with organoids 30–60 days — prefer “neonatal / early postnatal” in casual hooks; use paper range in technical copy.
11. **Applications soft-pedal:** Prefer “platform enabling circuit- and behavior-level readouts with human neurons” over “brain diseases solved.”
