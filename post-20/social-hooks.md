# Social hooks — post-20

## 5 share hooks

1. **Hook (curiosity gap):** You can’t ethically watch a living human cortex wire, fail under oxygen stress, or grow the rare cells that vanish in frontotemporal dementia. Stanford’s Pașca lab just published a workaround in *Nature*: clear most of a mouse cortex genetically, then fill the cavity with human cortical organoids. They call it **xenocortication**.

2. **Hook (counterintuitive science):** Prior organoid transplants had to fight an intact rodent cortex for turf. Apallial mice — Emx1-Cre deleting Esco2 in dorsal/medial pallium on SCID — grow up with ~**2%** normal cortical content. Into that empty developmental space went human organoids. Competition, not just culture conditions, was the bottleneck.

3. **Hook (stat punch):** *Nature*, **September 16, 2026** (DOI 10.1038/s41586-026-11032-2). Graft survival **86.2%** of 29 mice (3 hiPS lines). MRI: **4.7×** graft volume growth from month 2→3. At 3 months, human tissue = **91.9%** of combined cortical volume. Sparse human axons in the **cervical spinal cord** (every mouse examined). **VEN-like** cells in all 3 sampled XCX brains. Hypoxia (**5% O₂**, 5 h): **HIF1α** lit the human graft, not the mouse palaeocortex next door.

4. **Hook (stakes):** Cerebral palsy risk biology, profound autism, epilepsy, schizophrenia circuits, frontotemporal dementia cell classes — every one needs *human* tissue inside a living nervous system with a behavior scoreboard. This is still a **research platform**, not a mind transplant and not a cured patient. It is peer-reviewed space-making for human cortex in vivo.

5. **Hook (identity / shareability):** Not a BCI implant. Not BlueRock dopamine grafts. A Sunday developmental-neuroscience story: **make space first**, then watch human organoids become most of a cortical volume, sprout rare VEN-like cells no dish offered, and fail like human tissue when oxygen runs out. Caveats mandatory. The share line is still clean — human cortex, living host, measurable injury.

## Image caption tip

Prefer a three-panel MRI/schematic: control cortex → apallial cavity → xenocortical mouse with human graft occupying ~92% of cortical volume; optional inset of sparse STEM121⁺ axons in cervical cord or VEN-like morphology. Caption: *“Xenocortication — human organoids filled 91.9% of cortical volume; axons reached spinal cord.”* Avoid implying conscious humanized mice, restored human memory, or clinical therapies. Say “research platform / developmental model.”
