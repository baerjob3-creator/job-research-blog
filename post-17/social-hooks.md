# Social hooks — post-17

## 5 share hooks

1. **Hook (curiosity gap):** Empty space is not empty. It is a seething bath of quantum vacuum fluctuations. A USTC–Shanghai Jiao Tong–MIT team just showed those fluctuations can do something textbooks rarely promise: **strengthen superconductivity** — without shining a laser, doping the crystal, or squeezing it in a pressure cell.

2. **Hook (counterintuitive science):** Most cavity tricks blast matter with light. This cavity was **dark**. Layered NbSe₂ sat in a near-terahertz split-ring resonator that amplifies vacuum fluctuations alone. Same flake, two environments: inside the cavity, critical temperature rose up to **5.4%**; near the transition, critical current and critical field jumped too. No photons on the invoice.

3. **Hook (stat punch):** First experimental vacuum-fluctuation **enhancement** of superconductivity, published in *Nature* **August 19, 2026** (DOI 10.1038/s41586-026-11037-x). Six-layer NbSe₂: **T<sub>c</sub> +5.4%** max. Enhancement peaked when cavity frequency matched low-energy superconducting fluctuations — a resonant fingerprint that survives strain-and-fabrication rebuttals. Theory coauthors include **Frank Wilczek**. They call the program **vacuumtronics**.

4. **Hook (stakes):** Superconducting circuits, quantum sensors, and quantum processors already live inside engineered electromagnetic boxes. If shaping the vacuum can nudge operating margins without adding heat or chemical disorder, materials control gains a noninvasive knob — even while today’s percentage gains stay modest and cryogenic.

5. **Hook (identity / shareability):** Not room-temperature superconductivity. Not a Maglev redesign. A peer-reviewed proof that the “empty” background can be an actor: virtual-photon exchange with a dark cavity lowers the superconducting state’s energy. Sunday condensed-matter physics with the caveats in the same breath as the 5.4% — Wilczek’s line included: the vacuum stopped being scenery.

## Image caption tip

Prefer a clean schematic: few-layer NbSe₂ flake partially inside a terahertz split-ring / dark cavity → resistance-vs-temperature curves comparing in-cavity vs out-of-cavity regions of the same flake → resonant peak of ΔT<sub>c</sub> vs cavity frequency. Caption: *“Same crystal. Dark cavity. Vacuum fluctuations raised T<sub>c</sub> up to 5.4%.”* Avoid implying room-temperature superconductivity, net-energy devices, or immediate qubit products. Say “proof-of-principle / cryogenic NbSe₂ / first vacuum-enhancement report.”
