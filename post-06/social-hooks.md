# Social hooks — post-06

## 5 share hooks

1. **Hook (curiosity gap):** What if the correct answer to a math problem was also the lowest-energy state of a drop of salt water? Scientists just built that computer — out of DNA.

2. **Hook (counterintuitive science):** Most computers fight entropy. This one *uses* it. Maynooth’s Scaffolded DNA Computer (*Nature*, Sep 16, 2026) heats, cools, and relaxes into the right answer — no continuous electricity.

3. **Hook (stat punch):** 10 programs. 100-bit addition. 10 + 3 in ~30 seconds. 700+ computations. Reused dozens of times. One plate dried out — add water 1.5 years later, run it again.

4. **Hook (stakes):** Silicon and data centres already devour a huge share of national electricity. This DNA system will not replace your laptop — but it proves computation can be designed so the answer is the equilibrium.

5. **Hook (identity / shareability):** Somewhere in Ireland, a fridge full of DNA strands is a program library. Pick strands, add salt water, anneal — and the molecules settle into math.
