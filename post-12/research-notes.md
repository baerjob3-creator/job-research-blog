## Caveats for editors / Website Developer

1. **Investigational ≠ approved screen:** Prefer “investigational liquid biopsy / not a population screening test / may triage high-risk patients to imaging.” Do not imply FDA clearance or clinic-ready general screening.
2. **Sensitivity ≠ positive predictive value:** For rare PDAC, even ~87% sensitivity can yield poor PPV. Kamath’s Healio caveat belongs in body or editor notes.
3. **Cohort count wording:** Abstract says **1,785** individuals; Healio says **1,757** plasma samples from **1,649** unique people. Prefer abstract for headline cohort size; Healio for sample/unique distinction when precision matters.
4. **87% vs 86.8%:** Public releases round to 87%; paper figure is **86.8%** for stage I–II with CA19-9. Lead with 86.8% in technical passages; “~87%” OK in hooks if sourced as outreach rounding.
5. **miRNA panel size:** Abstract lists named miRNAs; outreach/Healio describe a **13-microRNA** signature (circulating + exosomal). Prefer “multi-miRNA / 13-miRNA signature” without inventing unlisted IDs beyond the abstract’s named set if enumerating.
6. **Survival stats:** City of Hope cites ~14% five-year; Healio cites ACS ~13% overall / ~3% distant / ~44% localized. Prefer ranges or ACS-attributed figures; do not invent a single “exact” survival number.
7. **False-positive asymmetry:** FPR higher in high-risk (15.6%) than low-risk (3.2%) — counterintuitive; Kamath flags it. Do not flatten to “low false positives” without the high-risk figure.
8. **Conflict of interest:** Goel is a paid consultant to Biocartis (option on PANXEON). Disclose when discussing commercialization path.
9. **Differentiation from post-01:** Virtual Biotech = AI *drug discovery* for cancer. This post = *early detection* liquid biopsy. Same disease family; different scientific lane.
10. **Quotes:** Goel quotes from City of Hope/EurekAlert/Healio are usable with attribution. Kamath perspective quotes usable with Healio attribution. Do not fabricate additional quotes.
