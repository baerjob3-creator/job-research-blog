# Social hooks — post-12

## 5 share hooks

1. **Hook (curiosity gap):** Pancreatic cancer usually wins by arriving late — ~90% of tumors are found after they’ve advanced. A City of Hope–led *Nature Medicine* blood test just flagged **stage I–II** disease in the testing cohort with **86.8%** sensitivity. The name: PANXEON.

2. **Hook (counterintuitive science):** One biomarker was never enough. PANXEON fuses **three** blood signals — free microRNAs, exosomal microRNAs, and CA19-9 — into a single AI risk score. The multi-signal combo, not a magic protein, is what moved early detection into clinically interesting range.

3. **Hook (stat punch):** Testing cohort: miRNA AUROC **88.6%**; early-stage sensitivity **83.8%** (miRNA alone) → **86.8%** with CA19-9; false positives **3.2%** (low-risk) / **15.6%** (high-risk); high-grade dysplasia in high-risk cysts **64.3%**. Cohort: ~1,785 people across the U.S., Europe, Asia. Paper: Xu, Goel et al., *Nat Med* (DOI 10.1038/s41591-026-04625-x). Public wave: City of Hope / EurekAlert + Healio Sep 16; Medical News Today Sep 17, 2026.

4. **Hook (stakes):** Five-year survival is ~13–14% overall and ~3% once disease is distant. Localized disease still only ~44%. A stage shift is not a cure by itself — but finding PDAC while surgery and new therapies still matter is the difference between a closed window and an open one.

5. **Hook (identity / shareability):** Not an approved population screen. Not a CT replacement. A triage blood score for people already under a pancreatic shadow — family risk, cysts, chronic pancreatitis — published in *Nature Medicine* and already being stress-tested in the oncology press on the right question: will the **positive predictive value** hold when the disease is rare? Sunday-night medicine that refuses to oversell.

## Image caption tip

Prefer City of Hope credit art from the EurekAlert release, or a clean schematic: blood tube → three channels (circulating miRNA / exosomal miRNA / CA19-9) → AI composite score → triage to imaging. Optional: simple stage I–II vs late-detection contrast graphic. Caption: *“Three blood signals. One risk score. A chance to catch pancreatic cancer earlier.”* Avoid claiming FDA approval, population screening readiness, or “cures pancreatic cancer.” Say “investigational / 86.8% sensitivity in testing cohort / warrants further prospective studies.”
