# Social hooks — post-11

## 5 share hooks

1. **Hook (curiosity gap):** Earth and Venus are near twins. One has a big moon climbing away at ~4 cm/year. The other has zero moons. New *ApJ* models say Venus didn’t need a second catastrophe — tides alone can make a planet **eat** its satellite.

2. **Hook (counterintuitive science):** More massive moons die *faster* around Venus. Outward migration scales ~∝ moon mass, but the synchronous radius expands ~∝ mass². Cross ~2 lunar masses or spin slower than ~15 hours post-impact, and Roche destruction arrives in as little as ~0.03–1.7 billion years.

3. **Hook (stat punch):** Parameter survey: P₀ = 5–100 hr; Mₘ = 0.01–10 lunar masses; P_crit ≈ 16.1 hr at 5 R_V. Lunar-mass moon can survive 4.5 Gyr if Venus spun ≲12 hr after impact — but giant-impact sims aiming at today’s rotation park that moon on the **survival boundary**. Paper: Kane et al., *ApJ* 1009:31 (DOI 10.3847/1538-4357/ae9d6c). Public wave: UCR Sep 14 → ScienceDaily Sep 16 → phys.org Sep 20, 2026.

4. **Hook (stakes):** A swallowed moon dumps energy and angular momentum into the planet — rotation, geology, climate, maybe early oceans. Finding the crater is hard: ~80% of Venus’s surface was resurfaced ~1 Gyr ago. The smoking gun, if any, may be **inside** the planet — and the same tidal math threatens slowly spinning “Earth twins” around other stars.

5. **Hook (identity / shareability):** Earth’s Moon is still drifting out. Venus may have kept the receipt for a moon that spiraled **in**. “Venus ate its moon” isn’t clickbait physics — it’s peer-reviewed tidal demise, and a Sunday-night reminder that habitability isn’t only about forming a satellite. It’s about spinning fast enough to **keep** one.

Prefer NASA/JPL-Caltech Venus volcanism art (Peter Rubin) or a clean schematic: fast-spin Earth → moon outward; slow/post-impact Venus → synchronous radius overtakes orbit → Roche spiral-in. Optional: Kane/UCR graph of moon-destruction likelihood if credited. Caption: *“Same size class. Opposite satellite fate.”* Avoid claiming “Venus definitely had a moon” or “243-day spin alone proves the crash.” Say “if formed, tides can remove it” / “catastrophic stripping not required.”
