## Caveats for editors / Website Developer

1. **Hypothesis ≠ detection:** Paper does **not** prove Venus once had a moon. Prefer “if formed / hypothetical / could have” language. Kane states formation remains open.
2. **Press vs paper nuance:** UCR/ScienceDaily emphasize today’s **243-day** slow spin and inward spiral. The peer-reviewed core is a **post-impact coupled spin–orbit survey** with a real **survival island** at P₀ ≲ 12 hr for lunar-mass moons. Do not flatten to “Venus’s present day always destroys any moon instantly.”
3. **Model dependence:** Constant-Q vs constant time-lag differ near synchronism; CTL allows quasi-synchronous survival pockets for some massive moons at fast spin. Lead with constant-Q headline numbers but note dual models.
4. **Mass survey wording:** Paper spans **0.01–10** M_Moon; some outreach says “half to ten times” for the varied runs — prefer the paper’s full range when citing the survey.
5. **Publication vs press dates:** *ApJ* published Sep 14, 2026 (journal date Sep 20); UCR Sep 14; ScienceDaily Sep 16; phys.org Sep 20. Prefer “peer-reviewed result amplified this week / same-day public wave” rather than “discovered today.”
6. **Resurfacing caveat:** ~80% / ~1 Gyr resurfacing argument is from outreach context for why surface evidence is hard — not a new Kane measurement of resurfacing age.
7. **Quotes:** Kane quotes in UCR/ScienceDaily (“didn’t require a catastrophe…,” “shocked,” Earth-twin moon question) are usable with attribution. Do not fabricate additional quotes.
8. **Credits for art:** NASA/JPL-Caltech/Peter Rubin Venus volcanism illustration (UCR); optional Kane/UCR destruction-likelihood graph with credit.
9. **DOI / arXiv:** 10.3847/1538-4357/ae9d6c and arXiv:2608.25036 belong in Sources.
10. **Differentiation:** Not JWST IC 348 (post-09) or any collider/CERN lane (posts #5, #10). This is Venus tidal dynamics / comparative planetology.
