## Caveats for editors / Website Developer

1. **Not a cure:** Never imply HIV is cured or that ART can be stopped outside a monitored trial.
2. **Cohort limits:** RIO randomized **adult males**; do not over-generalize to women, children, or all viral subtypes.
3. **160-week case = anecdote within trial:** Lead with it as proof-of-possibility; always pair with population fractions (20/48/72 weeks) and placebo contrast.
4. **Reservoir half-life is exploratory/associative:** Prefer “estimated half-life ~36 weeks in these analyses” — not definitive proof bNAbs alone clear the reservoir. Note defective proviruses did not show the same decline.
5. **Two papers:** Clinical time-to-rebound primary → *Lancet HIV*; mechanism/secondary outcomes → *Nature Medicine*. Do not collapse authorship or claims across them without labeling.
6. **Antibody IDs:** Use **3BNC117-LS** and **10-1074-LS** (long-acting LS variants). Rebound resistance pattern: typically resistant to 10-1074-LS while still vulnerable to 3BNC117-LS.
7. **Autologous Abs:** “Generally thought weak alone” is the institutional framing; the finding is synergistic help with infused bNAbs, not that autologous Abs cure HIV solo.
8. **Quotes:** Nussenzweig and Fumagalli quotes from Rockefeller/EurekAlert are usable with attribution. Do not fabricate additional quotes.
9. **Funding mention OK:** Gates Foundation; Rockefeller Stavros Niarchos Institute of Infectious Diseases.
10. **Differentiation from post-12:** Pancreatic liquid biopsy ≠ HIV bNAb immunotherapy. Same “medicine Sunday” family only at the highest level.
