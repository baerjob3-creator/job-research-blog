# Social hooks — post-14

## 5 share hooks

1. **Hook (curiosity gap):** Daily HIV meds suppress the virus — they don’t teach your immune system to hold it. Rockefeller’s RIO follow-up says **one or two long-acting antibody infusions** kept HIV undetectable for months after stopping ART — and in some people, control lasted **after the antibodies should have washed out**.

2. **Hook (counterintuitive science):** Patient-made anti-HIV antibodies were long written off as too weak alone. In RIO, people who already had them averaged **~108 weeks** off ART before restarting vs **~27.5 weeks** without them. Nussenzweig’s metaphor: infused bNAbs are two wheels of a **tricycle**; autologous antibodies are the stabilizing third.

3. **Hook (stat punch):** Phase II RIO: **68** men randomized to dual long-acting bNAbs (**3BNC117-LS** + **10-1074-LS**) or placebo, then monitored ART pause. Early readout: most bNAb recipients undetectable up to **~20 weeks**; half of second-dose group at **48 weeks**; ~one third at **72 weeks**. *Lancet HIV*: ~**75% vs 11%** avoided rebound at 20 weeks. Intact reservoir half-life estimate after bNAbs: **~36 weeks** vs **4–7 years** on ART. One participant still ART-free past **160 weeks**. Paper: Fumagalli, Nussenzweig et al., *Nature Medicine* (DOI 10.1038/s41591-026-04644-8). Press: Rockefeller / EurekAlert Sep 17, 2026.

4. **Hook (stakes):** A quieter intact reservoir is the difference between lifelong daily pills and durable remission strategies. If bNAbs can accelerate intact-provirus decline *and* recruit host immunity, the next designs write themselves: vaccinate for the third wheel, then dose smarter antibody combos that resist single-mAb escape.

5. **Hook (identity / shareability):** Not a cure press conference. Not permission to stop ART at home. A *Nature Medicine* secondary analysis of a randomized interruption trial: antibodies that may **teach** the immune system, a **36-week** reservoir half-life claim worth arguing over carefully, and one person quiet past three years. Sunday medicine that refuses to oversell the pharmacy timeline.

## Image caption tip

Prefer Rockefeller / Science Photo Library HIV illustration or a simple schematic: dual bNAb infusion → ART pause → optional second dose @ 20 weeks → autologous Ab “third wheel” → intact reservoir decay. Caption: *“Two infused antibodies, one homemade stabilizer — and months (sometimes years) without daily ART under trial monitoring.”* Avoid implying universal cure, unsupervised treatment interruption, or that every participant matches the 160-week case. Say “trial-monitored ART pause / exploratory reservoir analysis / not a licensed cure.”
