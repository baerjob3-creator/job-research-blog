## Caveats for editors / Website Developer

1. **Strong evidence ≠ 5σ “observation”:** Headline and body should prefer CERN/ATLAS language (“strong evidence,” “4.7 standard deviations”) over “proven” or “discovered entanglement for the first time in history.”
2. **Not a loophole-free Bell test:** CMS is explicit that direct Z spin polarimetry is unavailable; lepton-angle reconstruction yields a polarization density matrix. Do not claim a textbook Bell inequality closure.
3. **SM assumptions in the sharpest test:** ATLAS’s complementary likelihood-ratio test “relying on several Standard-Model assumptions in the decays” — note this when summarizing the 4.7σ figure.
4. **Publication vs press dates:** CMS feature Mar 23, 2026; ATLAS briefing Jun 5, 2026; PRL ~Sep 11, 2026 (DOI 10.1103/y1nh-1b82); CERN Home Sep 17, 2026. Prefer “peer-reviewed ATLAS result amplified this week” rather than “discovered today.”
5. **Prior LHC entanglement:** Top-quark entanglement (ATLAS, 2023) exists — this post’s novelty is **massive vector bosons / Z qutrits from Higgs**, not “first entanglement at LHC.”
6. **No FTL / no teleportation:** Entanglement here is correlation structure, not signaling. Keep popular hooks honest.
7. **Quotes:** Prefer CERN/ATLAS/CMS published phrasing; do not fabricate researcher quotes absent from sourced pieces. CMS quotes (Huang, Pinto, Davis) are usable if attributed.
8. **Credits for art:** ATLAS Collaboration/CERN event displays; artistic impression Daniel Dominguez/CERN (per CERN Home).
9. **DOI / arXiv:** 10.1103/y1nh-1b82 and arXiv:2603.26463 belong in Sources.
10. **Differentiation from post-05:** Post-05 = BASE-STEP antimatter *transport* logistics. Post-10 = Higgs → ZZ *quantum correlations*. Same lab campus; different physics.
