# Social hooks — post-10

## 5 share hooks

1. **Hook (curiosity gap):** The Higgs boson has spin zero. The two Z bosons it produces somehow still know each other’s spin state. CERN says that’s not a coincidence — it’s quantum entanglement at collider energies.

2. **Hook (counterintuitive science):** Entanglement isn’t only for photons in a lab. ATLAS just reported **4.7-sigma** evidence that massive Z bosons born from a Higgs decay form an entangled pair — reconstructed from four leptons in the LHC’s “golden channel.”

3. **Hook (stat punch):** C₂,₁,₂,₋₁ = −0.71 ± 0.45. Likelihood test vs separable hypothesis: **4.7σ** observed (4.9 expected). Fiducial Higgs cross section still matches the Standard Model: 3.65 ± 0.35 fb vs 3.68 ± 0.17 fb. Paper: *Phys. Rev. Lett.* Public wave: CERN Home, Sep 17, 2026.

4. **Hook (stakes):** Tops were entangled at the LHC in 2023. Z bosons are different beasts — spin-1 qutrits, not qubits. If the weak force’s own messenger shows spooky correlations, the Higgs isn’t just a mass-giver. It’s a quantum-information factory.

5. **Hook (identity / shareability):** Einstein hated “spooky action.” Geneva just put it on a Higgs. Two Z bosons. Four leptons. One entangled system — and a Sunday-night physics headline that doesn’t need a cartoon particle to travel.
