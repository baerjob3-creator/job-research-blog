# Social hooks — post-16

## 5 share hooks

1. **Hook (curiosity gap):** Parkinson’s drugs replace missing dopamine. They do not replace the **neurons** that made it. BlueRock and Memorial Sloan Kettering’s bemdaneprocel trial did the harder thing: graft **stem-cell–derived dopamine neuron progenitors** into both sides of the putamen — then watch what happens after the immune drugs stop.

2. **Hook (counterintuitive science):** Allogeneic brain grafts usually mean lifelong immunosuppression — or rejection. In *Nature*’s Phase I exPDite readout, participants got basiliximab, prednisone, and tacrolimus for **only one year**. At **18 months** — six months off those drugs — **¹⁸F-DOPA PET** still showed rising putaminal signal. The grafts did not vanish when the pharmacy cover ended.

3. **Hook (stat punch):** Twelve people. Low dose **0.9M** cells/putamen (*n*=5) vs high dose **2.7M** (*n*=7). Primary safety endpoint cleared at 12 months: **zero** cell-product–related AEs, **zero** graft-induced dyskinesias. At 18 months, high-dose MDS-UPDRS Part III OFF improved by a mean **23.0** points; good ON time **+2.7 h/day**; OFF time **−2.7 h/day**. October 2025 update: high-dose OFF improvement still **17.9** points at **36 months**. DOI 10.1038/s41586-025-08845-y; NCT04802733.

4. **Hook (stakes):** More than **10 million** people live with Parkinson’s; projections push past **14 million** by 2040. Fetal midbrain grafts once teased a cellular fix, then hit blinded-trial and dyskinesia walls. An **off-the-shelf, GMP hES-derived** product that survives past year-one immunosuppression is the manufacturing answer those trials never had — if Phase III with sham surgery confirms the motor signal.

5. **Hook (identity / shareability):** Not a cure press conference. Not “stop your Sinemet.” A peer-reviewed Phase I plus a three-year company durability update: cryopreserved dopamine progenitors, dose-tilted motor gains, PET that stayed lit after immune drugs quit, and a pivotal trial designed to falsify the hope. Sunday regenerative neurology that keeps the caveats in the same paragraph as the 23-point headline.

Prefer a clean schematic: hES cells → floor-plate DA progenitors (bemdaneprocel) → bilateral stereotactic putamen grafts → 12-month immunosuppression then stop → ¹⁸F-DOPA PET + MDS-UPDRS / PD diary timeline to 18 and 36 months.
