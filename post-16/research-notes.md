## Caveats for editors / Website Developer

1. **Not efficacy-proven:** Emphasize open-label, non-randomized, no sham control; Phase I primary endpoint was **safety**, not efficacy. Phase III (exPDite-2) is the confirmatory bar.
2. **Do not say “cured” or “off meds”:** Medication use remained similar to baseline at 18 months; diary/scale gains are not a license to stop levodopa.
3. **18-month peer-reviewed vs 36-month company/congress update:** Keep *Nature* as the primary clinical citation; attribute 36-month numbers to BlueRock/Bayer Oct 7, 2025 release / MDS presentation and note smaller diary completeness (e.g., two high-dose participants missing 36-month PD Diary).
4. **Score drift:** High-dose OFF improvement was **23.0** at 18 months and **17.9** at 36 months — report both honestly; do not pretend the peak locked forever.
5. **Immunosuppression contrast with post-15:** Bemdaneprocel **used** one year of immune drugs then stopped; UP421 used **zero**. Do not blur the two transplant philosophies.
6. **Product IDs:** Use **bemdaneprocel** (BRT-DA01 / formerly MSK-DA01); trial nickname **exPDite**; cells are **hES-derived dopaminergic progenitors**, not iPS in this paper (separate iPS PD programs exist elsewhere — do not conflate).
7. **Dose units:** Cells are dosed **per putamen** (bilateral), not total body — 0.9M and 2.7M per side.
8. **GID honesty:** “No graft-induced dyskinesias to date” in this small cohort — do not claim the risk is abolished forever.
9. **Quotes:** Henchcliffe cautionary line from the Oct 2025 Bayer release is usable with attribution (“need for caution… Phase III should shed further light”). Do not fabricate patient testimonials.
10. **BCI adjacency:** Post-02 was speech BCI; this is biological DA replacement. No “same brain implant story” framing.
