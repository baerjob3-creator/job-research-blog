When digging into a new topic, it helps to separate intake from judgment. First gather sources and note what they claim; then decide what holds up.

A simple loop

  1. Question — Write one sentence naming what you're trying to learn.
  2. Scan — Skim a few sources for claims and evidence, not conclusions yet.
  3. Synthesize — In your own words, say what seems solid, what is contested, and what is still unknown.
  4. Share — Turn the note into a short post so the next reader (or future you) gets the gist quickly.

Why post it?

Publishing a research note — even a short one — forces clarity. Gaps show up when you write for someone else. That is the point of this blog: make the research trail visible and useful.
