# Social hooks — post-03

## 5 share hooks

1. **Hook (curiosity gap):** Scientists just confirmed all five letters of DNA and RNA inside asteroid Ryugu — the complete genetic alphabet, sealed and returned from space. The contamination excuse is dead.

2. **Hook (counterintuitive science):** Asteroids don’t ship one genetic starter kit. Ryugu, Bennu, and Orgueil brew different purine/pyrimidine mixes — and ammonia appears to write the recipe (R² = 0.89).

3. **Hook (stat punch):** From ~5.4 grams of Hayabusa2 dust: adenine, guanine, cytosine, thymine, *and* uracil. One sample hit ~1,577 pmol of nucleobases per gram. Tiny amounts. Huge implication.

4. **Hook (mission arc):** 2023 found uracil in Ryugu. 2025 found all five letters in Bennu. March 2026 closed Ryugu’s set. Two sample-return missions. Same alphabet. Not a fluke.

5. **Hook (identity / shareability):** The alphabet of life may have been written in space long before anything on Earth learned how to read — and we now have the pristine receipts.

## Image caption tip

Use JAXA Hayabusa2 imagery of Ryugu (colored surface mosaic) or a clean lab photo of sealed sample grains — **not** DNA double-helix stock art alone, which overclaims “life found.” Better: split graphic of the five nucleobase structures labeled A/G/C/T/U beside Ryugu. Caption: *“All five genetic letters. One asteroid. Zero Earth contamination.”* Attribute JAXA/JAMSTEC; avoid inventing glowing green “alien DNA” visuals.
