2. **1-D+ hydrodynamics:** GR1D + STIR turbulence surrogate; multi-D effects and progenitor asymmetries can change explodability islands.
3. **High-ρc failure fractions:** ~74–88% IMF-weighted may conflict with observational caps; authors flag this and call for dynamical subgrid FC models.
4. **Publication vs press dates:** arXiv May 2026; PRD accepted Aug 5, 2026; Space.com Sep 20, 2026. Prefer “accepted / published in PRD; amplified Sep 20” rather than “discovered today.”
5. **Dual effect:** FC can *increase* failures overall *and* (near the shock) accelerate revival → lighter NSs. Do not flatten to “neutrinos only kill explosions.”
6. **Quotes:** Only use Gogilashvili and Tamborra lines from Space.com — do not fabricate.
7. **Affiliation:** Niels Bohr International Academy and DARK, Niels Bohr Institute, University of Copenhagen.
8. **DOI / arXiv:** 10.1103/pz3y-3lv5 and arXiv:2605.16504 — both belong in Sources.
