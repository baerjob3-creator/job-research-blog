# Social hooks — post-08

## 5 share hooks

1. **Hook (curiosity gap):** Massive stars dump ~99% of their death energy into invisible neutrinos. New Copenhagen simulations say those “ghost particles” may also decide whether the star explodes — or quietly collapses into a black hole.

2. **Hook (counterintuitive science):** Changing a neutrino’s *flavor* can flip a dying star’s fate. In 195 collapse simulations, flavor conversion made 16–30 solar-mass stars far more likely to fail as supernovas — the same mass range where astronomy’s “missing explosions” mystery lives.

3. **Hook (stat punch):** No flavor conversion: ~27% failed explosions (IMF-weighted). With it: 47.6% → 88.3%, depending on where conversion fires. Paper: *Physical Review D*. Public wave: Space.com, Sep 20, 2026.

4. **Hook (stakes):** Too few supernovas. Red supergiants that vanish. Neutron stars lighter than theory likes. One microphysics upgrade — neutrino identity swaps in the core — may ease all three cosmic bookkeeping problems.

5. **Hook (identity / shareability):** The universe’s quietest messengers may also be its quietest executioners. Flavor-changing neutrinos: the veto vote on whether a dying star gets fireworks or a black hole.
