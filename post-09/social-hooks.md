# Social hooks — post-09

## 5 share hooks

1. **Hook (curiosity gap):** Webb found a free-floating object that weighs about *twice Jupiter* — but it was born in a star nursery, not a planetary system. Its mass says planet. Its birth certificate says star.

2. **Hook (counterintuitive science):** The least massive known brown dwarf with a disk (LRL 2296, ~2 Jupiter masses) may hold the raw materials to build smaller worlds — around an object that itself has only a planet’s weight.

3. **Hook (stat punch):** 39 candidates → 15 spectra → 9 new IC 348 members. Faintest confirmed floor: ~2 M_Jup (~0.19% of the Sun). Paper: *ApJL*. Public wave: NASA panorama Sep 15 + SpaceDaily Sep 20, 2026.

4. **Hook (stakes):** If star formation can isolate only two Jupiters of material — and leave a disk — then “planet vs brown dwarf” is not a weighing contest. It’s a formation story. Webb just made the argument public.

5. **Hook (identity / shareability):** Where does a planet end and a star begin? In Perseus, Webb blurred the line — and proposed a new spectral class “H” for the coolest newborns wearing mystery hydrocarbons.

Alternate: simple schematic — molecular cloud collapse → free-floating ~2 M_Jup object + IR-excess disk → fork labeled “planet by mass / brown dwarf by birth.” Caption: *“Planet weight. Star birth. Maybe a system of its own.”* Avoid claiming Webb resolved baby planets in the disk, or that masses are dynamical (they are evolutionary-model estimates).
