## Caveats for editors / Website Developer

1. **Model-dependent masses:** ~2 M_Jup from luminosity + age + evolutionary tracks, not dynamical orbits. Paper notes tracks are least secure in this regime; qualitative “few Jupiter masses” conclusion is robust to large systematic error.
2. **Disk ≠ resolved planets:** IR excess / ice absorption only; no claim of detected forming planets or resolved rings.
3. **Formation preference ≠ proof:** Spatial statistics + disk favor cloud collapse over ejection; age ~5 Myr could weaken the spatial discriminant.
4. **Publication vs press dates:** *ApJL* published June 10, 2025; NASA panorama Sep 15, 2026; SpaceDaily Sep 20, 2026. Prefer “peer-reviewed discovery amplified this week” rather than “discovered today.”
5. **Hydrocarbon identity:** Feature attributed to unidentified aliphatic hydrocarbon; “H” class is a proposal defined by the 3.4 μm band — not a completed IAU taxonomy rewrite.
6. **Quotes:** Prefer NASA/SpaceDaily framing; do not fabricate researcher quotes not present in sourced pieces.
7. **Credits for art:** NASA, ESA, CSA, Kevin Luhman (PSU), Catarina Alves de Oliveira (ESA), Mahdi Zamani (ESA/Webb).
8. **DOI / arXiv:** 10.3847/2041-8213/addc55 and arXiv:2506.08969 belong in Sources.
