# Social hooks — post-13

## 5 share hooks

1. **Hook (curiosity gap):** Silicon solar cells are bumping into a ~29% physical ceiling. A Soochow–LONGi team just stacked perovskite on silicon with a trick that sounds too small to matter — **specks of zirconia** — and hit **34.0%** lab efficiency plus a certified **2.014 V** open-circuit voltage.

2. **Hook (counterintuitive science):** The old solar trade-off: passivate the buried interface harder and you choke charge extraction. Discrete **ZrO₂** nanoparticles refuse the choice — field-effect passivation *and* open SAM pathways for holes. Carrier lifetime nearly doubled (**1.46 → 2.81 μs**).

3. **Hook (stat punch):** Champion cell: **34.0%** PCE, Voc **1.997 V**, Jsc **20.36 mA cm⁻²**, FF **83.62%**. Independent cert: Voc **2.014 V**, steady-state **33.5%**. After **2,000 h** continuous 1-sun MPPT: **84%** of initial efficiency retained (control without the scaffold: **70%** by 1,000 h). Paper: Zhang, Zheng, Mo, Liu, Zhang, He et al., *Science Bulletin* (DOI 10.1016/j.scib.2026.09.007). Press wave: *pv magazine* Sep 10; EurekAlert Sep 18; Interesting Engineering Sep 20, 2026.

4. **Hook (stakes):** Every tenth of a percent on a tandem is cheaper clean electricity at planetary scale. Silicon alone is asymptotically hitting its Auger wall. Tandems are how PV keeps compounding — but only if the joint between the two cells stops eating voltage. That joint just got quieter.

5. **Hook (identity / shareability):** Not the absolute world-record tandem (LONGi separately holds **35.5%** ESTI-certified). Not a rooftop panel shipping Monday. A *Science Bulletin* mechanism story: dual-anchored zirconia nano-scaffold → denser perovskite film → record-class voltage → better operational stability. Sunday energy that refuses to oversell the factory timeline.
