# Social hooks — post-15

## 5 share hooks

1. **Hook (curiosity gap):** Islet transplants for type 1 diabetes usually come with a lifetime of immune-suppressing drugs — or they get rejected. Uppsala and Sana’s UP421 case did something different: **CRISPR-edited donor islets** injected into a forearm muscle **with zero immunosuppressants**. The fully engineered cells never triggered the immune assays that destroyed the unedited ones in the same graft.

2. **Hook (counterintuitive science):** Strip HLA class I/II (B2M + CIITA knockouts) and the adaptive system loses its barcode — but innate NK cells and macrophages can still kill “missing-self.” The fix: **overexpress CD47**, the “don’t eat me” checkpoint. In one man, wild-type cells were rejected, double-knockouts were innately killed, and **HIP cells survived untouched for 12 weeks** — then still showed C-peptide and PET signal at **14 months**.

3. **Hook (stat punch):** First-in-human *NEJM* case (Carlsson et al., DOI 10.1056/NEJMoa2503822): **42**-year-old man, **37** years of type 1, baseline C-peptide **zero**, HbA1c **10.9%**. Product UP421: **85.8%** HLA-I neg, **100%** HLA-II neg, **46.4%** high CD47. Dose: **79.6 million** cells in **17** brachioradialis injections — intentionally ~**7%** of an insulin-independence mass. Stable glucose-responsive C-peptide through **12 weeks**; Sana **March 13, 2026** update: function and graft imaging through **14 months**, no immunosuppression. Nature news: Ledford, Sep 5, 2025.

4. **Hook (stakes):** ~Two million Americans live with type 1 diabetes. The unfinished transplant dream is not “more insulin pumps” — it is **living beta cells that the host immune system ignores**. If hypoimmune edits scale from a safety dose to a full dose *and* move onto stem-cell islets, the immunosuppression tax that kept most patients off transplant could finally get optional.

5. **Hook (identity / shareability):** Not a cure press conference. Not permission to stop insulin at home. A single-participant *NEJM* proof-of-concept plus a **14-month** company durability update: gene-edited donor islets that hid in plain sight, made meal-responsive insulin, and rewrote the transplant bargain from “suppress the host” to “edit the graft.” Sunday regenerative medicine that refuses to oversell the pharmacy timeline.
