## Caveats for editors / Website Developer

1. **Not a cure / not insulin-free:** Never imply the patient stopped insulin or that type 1 diabetes is cured. Emphasize intentional **low/safety dose (~7%)**.
2. **n = 1:** Single participant in the published *NEJM* case; do not generalize to women, children, or all disease durations without labeling.
3. **12-week peer-reviewed vs 14-month company update:** Keep the *NEJM* paper as the primary clinical citation; attribute 14-month durability to Sana’s March 13, 2026 release and note it is follow-up on the same study, not a second randomized trial.
4. **HbA1c drop:** Paper states the ~42% HbA1c reduction over 12 weeks was **probably entirely** from increased exogenous insulin to protect the graft — do not credit the graft alone for that metabolic improvement.
5. **Product heterogeneity:** UP421 contained HIP, DKO, and wild-type cells; immune attack on WT/DKO is a feature of the internal control narrative, not a failure of the whole product.
6. **Editing IDs:** Use **Cas12b** (not Cas9); genes **B2M** and **CIITA**; **CD47** via lentivirus; product **UP421**; platform **HIP** (hypoimmune).
7. **Site:** Left **brachioradialis** intramuscular — not portal-vein liver infusion (standard clinical islet route).
8. **Quotes:** Carlsson SciAm quote usable with attribution; Alonso/Herold cautionary quotes useful for balance. Do not fabricate additional quotes.
9. **Differentiation from post-14:** HIV bNAb remission ≠ diabetes hypoimmune islets. Do not cross-link as “same story.”
10. **Vertex adjacency:** Mention Vertex stem-cell islets only as contrast (insulin-free possible **with** immunosuppression) — do not conflate programs.
