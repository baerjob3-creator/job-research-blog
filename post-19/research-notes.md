## Caveats for editors / Website Developer

1. **Not a therapy / not in vivo:** Ex vivo electroporation into cell lines and primary cells only. Never imply patient dosing or approved CAR-T products from this paper.
2. **Standard PA ≠ nuclease PA:** Kilobase installs are framed without obligatory nuclease DSBs; megabase rearrangements used a nuclease-PA variant that *does* cut. Keep modes separate in titles/hooks.
3. **Differentiate from post-15:** Islets / immunosuppression / insulin ≠ large-payload genome engineering platform.
4. **HSPC efficiencies are modest:** 1.3–1.5% PA allele is proof-of-applicability, not clinical-ready yield. Do not inflate.
5. **Efficiency falls with donor size:** 28.1% at 9.1 kb vs 7.4% at 12.1 kb — quote both or the size range honestly.
6. **Off-targets not zero:** Donor-seq found guide-dependent events (e.g., TRAC/AAVS1 pegRNA sets). Prefer “high specificity vs nuclease knock-in” over “no off-targets.”
7. **Field convergence:** April 2026 Liu et al. and other 2026 donor-annealing papers exist — do not claim sole invention of large prime-edit inserts.
8. **Palbociclib assay nuance:** Paper notes fractional S/G2 progression after prolonged PD; HDR still collapsed. Do not overclaim “100% G1 forever.”
9. **TRAC / CAR language:** Prefer “docking locus relevant to CAR-T engineering” over “they built a CAR-T drug.”
10. **Applications soft-pedal:** Prefer “preclinical platform for medium-to-large genomic installs” over “gene therapy solved.”
