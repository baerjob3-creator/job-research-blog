# Social hooks — post-19

## 5 share hooks

1. **Hook (curiosity gap):** Gene therapy’s unfinished sentence: put a *large* DNA change exactly where you want it — without shredding the chromosome or waiting for the cell to divide. A Boston Children’s / Dana-Farber team just published **prime assembly** in *Nature*: human cells that assemble and park **gene-sized DNA** at an RNA-programmed address.

2. **Hook (counterintuitive science):** Homology-directed repair mostly works in dividing cells and usually leans on nuclease cuts. Prime assembly uses CRISPR **dual 3′ flaps** so donors anneal like a **Gibson assembly** *inside* the cell. In G1-arrested cells, HDR at AAVS1 went to undetectable; PA’s eGFP integration barely blinked.

3. **Hook (stat punch):** *Nature*, **September 16, 2026** (DOI 10.1038/s41586-026-11024-2). Donors up to **12.1 kb** at AAVS1 (**~28%** eGFP⁺ at 9.1 kb; **~7.4%** at 12.1 kb). Up to **four** ssDNA fragments assembled on target. Primary **CD3⁺ T cells** and **CD34⁺ HSPCs**. Nuclease-PA mode: **93 Mb** / **27 Mb** arm deletions; **1 Mb** inversion in **40.4%** of clones vs **12.5%** for SpCas9 NHEJ.

4. **Hook (stakes):** CAR docking at **TRAC**, multi-exon fixes, gene-sized cassettes — every therapy that needs more than a short edit hits the same wall. PA is still **ex vivo / preclinical**, not a cured patient. It is a peer-reviewed platform that moves the wall: large payloads, primary blood cells, no obligatory break for standard installs.

5. **Hook (identity / shareability):** Not another clinical CRISPR vignette. Not “DNA computing in a droplet.” A Sunday-methods story: **flaps instead of S-phase HDR**, **assembly instead of blunt knock-in**, megabase rearrangements when you deliberately add a nuclease. Caveats mandatory. The share line is still clean — human cells can paste chapters, not just letters.

## Image caption tip

Prefer a clean schematic: twin pegRNAs → dual 3′ flaps → overlapping ssDNA/dsDNA donor annealing → integrated gene-sized cassette at AAVS1 / TRAC / IL2RG; optional inset “G1-arrested: HDR ↓ / PA holds.” Caption: *“Prime assembly — up to 12.1 kb on target; works without cell division.”* Avoid implying in-vivo cures, approved CAR-T products, or “no off-targets ever.” Say “preclinical platform / ex vivo primary cells.”
