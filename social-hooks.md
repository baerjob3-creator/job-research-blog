## 3) LinkedIn — professional framing
Most “AI drug discovery” demos are a single model scoring a molecule.

Stanford just published something different in *Science*: a Virtual Biotech — a full multi-agent R&D org with a CSO agent, specialist divisions, and 37,000+ trialist agents.

They didn’t just generate hype. They surfaced single-cell features associated with trial success and independently proposed a B7-H3 antibody–drug conjugate approach later echoed by a therapy that received FDA breakthrough designation.

The authors are clear: this expands human scientists’ reach; it doesn’t replace wet labs or trials.

Worth a read if you work in biotech, AI, or clinical development.
