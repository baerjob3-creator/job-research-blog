# Social hooks — post-07

## 5 share hooks

1. **Hook (curiosity gap):** Seawater usually ruins green-hydrogen gadgets. So how did Singapore scientists build an artificial leaf that drinks dirty seawater, makes clean hydrogen, and destroys a toxic rocket-fuel chemical at the same time?

2. **Hook (counterintuitive science):** No external power. Hydrogen at *both* electrodes. Hydrazine from ~1.6% down to 0.5 ppb in ~30 hours. NTU’s sunlight-powered “artificial leaf” turns a pollution problem into the chemistry that unlocks seawater hydrogen.

3. **Hook (stat punch):** 25 mA cm⁻² photocurrent. >72 hours under 1-sun. 466 μmol H₂ cm⁻² h⁻¹. 0.5 M hydrazine → 0.5 ppb. EPA limit: 10 ppb. Peer-reviewed in *Nature Communications*; NTU wave Sep 16, 2026.

4. **Hook (stakes):** Green hydrogen’s quiet tax is clean water and electricity. A device that detoxifies industrial wastewater *while* making fuel changes where climate tech can earn its keep.

5. **Hook (identity / shareability):** Nature already invented leaves that eat sunlight. Engineers just taught one to eat toxic seawater and exhale hydrogen. Climate tech with a side hustle: cleanup.
