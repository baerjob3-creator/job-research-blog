## Caveats for editors / Website Developer

1. **Lab prototype, not a plant:** Do not imply commercial desalination replacement, municipal wastewater deployment, or grid-scale hydrogen today.
2. **Hydrazine is the intentional anode reactant:** The demo uses hydrazine-spiked seawater/wastewater chemistry. Do not blur into “any dirty ocean water works unchanged.”
3. **Publication vs press dates:** Paper VoR May 2026 (*Nature Communications*); NTU institutional highlight Sep 16, 2026; trade coverage Sep 17–18. Prefer “published earlier; highlighted mid-September” rather than “discovered today.”
4. **Endpoint wording:** Abstract: near-complete degradation to **below 1 ppb** in ≈30 h. NTU release: **0.5 ppb**. Keep both or prefer “below 1 ppb (NTU: 0.5 ppb).”
5. **Lead-halide perovskite:** Moisture/toxicity/stability caveats belong in “what it is not.” Protective epoxy + Ti foil is engineering, not a solved product.
6. **Quotes:** Only use Wong and Durrant lines from NTU — do not fabricate. Durrant was *not* involved in the research.
7. **Author roles:** Mahmoud G. Ahmed = first author (PubMed/search); Lydia H. Wong = lead / corresponding institutional face; collaborators Lie, Sadhu, Hao named by NTU.
8. **Patent:** Singapore provisional application noted on PubMed competing-interests — mention as “filed,” not “granted.”
9. **Chlorine suppression:** Important differentiator vs seawater OER demos — keep accurate; do not claim zero salt issues forever at scale.
