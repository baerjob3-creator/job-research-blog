# Social hooks — post-02

## 5 share hooks

1. **Hook (curiosity gap):** Most BCIs restore speech *or* movement. A UCSF team just restored both at once — from a single brain implant — and the science twist is wilder than the demo.

2. **Hook (human story):** Two people with paralysis waved hello *and* said it through the same virtual avatar, in the same session, from one cortical array. Peer-reviewed Sept 14, 2026.

3. **Hook (counterintuitive science):** Your brain doesn’t just add “speech + gesture.” Simultaneous talk-and-wave is its own neural pattern — and training BCIs as if it weren’t quietly breaks them.

4. **Hook (stat punch):** Before smarter training, false “wrong modality” firings hit 76% for one participant. After cross-training: zero. Same implant. Different data strategy.

5. **Hook (identity / shareability):** Losing body language can flatten a person as much as losing words. This BCI doesn’t just restore vocabulary — it puts the wave, nod, and shrug back in the conversation.

## Image caption tip

Use a still of a full-body virtual avatar mid-wave with on-screen speech text (or a clean diagram of a surface ECoG array over motor cortex). Caption: *“One implant. Speech + gesture. Same session.”* Avoid Neuralink-style thread imagery — this study used a surface ECoG array, not penetrating threads. Prefer NIH/UCSF/illustrative science art with attribution; do not invent participant faces.
