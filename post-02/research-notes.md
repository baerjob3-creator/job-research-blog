## Caveats for editors / Website Developer

1. **Small n / early feasibility:** Concurrent avatar work in **two** participants; overall trial enrollment three (one withdrew before avatar experiments). Do not imply large clinical efficacy.
2. **Limited vocab & structured tasks:** Conversation blocks were few; phrases/gestures curated. Avoid “they can talk freely about anything” framing.
3. **Wired ECoG, not consumer BCI:** Surface array + percutaneous pedestal + external processors. Chang has stated a wireless successor is next — that is prospective, not delivered.
4. **Not Neuralink / Utah / Stentrode:** Hardware platform differs; do not conflate in headlines or images.
5. **Accuracy context:** Report conversational and concurrent numbers with chance rates and “proof of concept” framing; prefer medians/means as published, not rounded marketing claims.
6. **Quotes:** Chang and Tucci quotes taken from NIH/EurekAlert release; verify against author-info / paper if tightening for legal.
7. **Competitor fairness:** Prior speech-or-movement decoding elsewhere is acknowledged; the novel claim is simultaneous single-implant single-session real-time multimodal decoding.
8. **Withdrawal reason for Bravo-3:** Not disclosed in public materials reviewed — do not invent one.
