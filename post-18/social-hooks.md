# Social hooks — post-18

## 5 share hooks

1. **Hook (curiosity gap):** Half the nitrogen in your body likely passed through a Haber–Bosch plant. A Bordeaux team just showed another way to make ammonia: **air or seawater + iron salts + a molybdenum catalyst + a UVC lamp** — at room temperature, with water as the electron donor. No fossil-hydrogen furnace in the flask.

2. **Hook (counterintuitive science):** Most “photochemical ammonia” recipes cheat with energetic sacrificial reductants. This one is **photosynthetic**: the net reaction is endergonic by **329.2 kJ mol⁻¹**. Light does thermodynamic work. Fe(II) makes solvated electrons under 254-nm light; a Mo catalyst reduces N₂; Fe(III) regenerates while oxidizing water. Same chemistry still runs in **unpurified seawater** and **ambient air**.

3. **Hook (stat punch):** *Nature Chemistry*, **August 25, 2026** (DOI 10.1038/s41557-026-02218-2). Photochemical quantum yield **5.0%** at normal T and P; **10.8%** under elevated N₂ pressure; **7.6%** light-to-chemical energy conversion. Authors: Zidouhia, Tassaing, Penin, Grau, Bassani (Université de Bordeaux / CNRS). News & Views landed **September 17, 2026**.

4. **Hook (stakes):** Industrial ammonia underwrites fertilizer — and about **1.8% of greenhouse-gas emissions**, per the paper’s framing. Every credible water-coupled, light-driven alternative is a food-security and climate story at once. This is not a plant. It is a peer-reviewed mechanism and quantum-yield bar that prior homogeneous systems rarely cleared without harsh reductants.

5. **Hook (identity / shareability):** Not sunlight-ready yet (UVC, not visible). Not Haber–Bosch retired. A Sunday-chemistry proof that you can ask N₂ for ammonia gently — iron for electrons, molybdenum for nitrogen, water for both protons and the oxidative half — and get a headline quantum yield without inventing a megaton factory. Seawater optional. Air optional. Caveats mandatory.

## Image caption tip

Prefer a clean schematic: N₂ (air or pressurized) + H₂O → NH₃ + O₂ under 254-nm lamp, with Fe(II)/Fe(III) shuttle and Mo–PCP catalyst highlighted; optional inset of seawater flask vs purified water. Caption: *“Room temperature. Water as electron donor. Quantum yield 5% at 1 bar — 10.8% under pressurized N₂.”* Avoid implying commercial fertilizer plants, sunlight-only operation, or immediate Haber–Bosch replacement. Say “lab photosynthetic ammonia / UVC / proof-of-principle.”
