## Caveats for editors / Website Developer

1. **Not Haber–Bosch replacement:** Lab quantum yields and flask concentrations ≠ megaton fertilizer plants. Never imply commercial displacement.
2. **UVC ≠ sunlight:** Photochemistry uses **254-nm** lamps. Do not claim “just leave it in the sun.” Visible-light redesign is future work.
3. **Differentiate from post-07:** That was **H₂** from seawater. This is **NH₃** from N₂ with water oxidation. Title/hooks must say ammonia / nitrogen fixation.
4. **Photosynthetic vs photocatalytic:** Authors’ key framing — endergonic net reaction, water as terminal donor. Keep that distinction; do not call every light+ammonia paper “photosynthesis.”
5. **Mo catalyst still required:** Iron is the photoelectron shuttle; Mo does N₂ activation. Do not say “iron alone fixes nitrogen.”
6. **Pressure caveat:** 10.8% QY needs elevated N₂ because of solubility; 5.0% is the ambient-pressure headline.
7. **0.28 g/L / 19 h:** Comes from first-author public communication around publication; useful color, secondary to peer-reviewed QY numbers — attribute carefully if used in ads.
8. **GHG 1.8%:** Use as paper/abstract framing for ammonia production’s climate share; do not invent tighter IEA figures unless citing IEA separately.
9. **Isotope / mechanism:** ¹⁵N₂ and H₂¹⁸O support net stoichiometry; transient spectroscopy supports Mo quenching of solvated electrons — keep mechanism language at that fidelity.
10. **Applications soft-pedal:** Prefer “proof-of-principle for water-coupled photosynthetic NH₃” over “green fertilizer solved.”
