# Social hooks — post-05

## 5 share hooks

1. **Hook (curiosity gap):** Antimatter annihilates if it touches matter. So how did scientists put 92 antiprotons on a truck, drive them across CERN, and bring every one home?

2. **Hook (counterintuitive science):** The first road trip for antimatter is not sci-fi — it is *Nature* (Sep 16, 2026). BASE-STEP: 92 antiprotons, ~8 km, zero loss, vacuum colder than almost anything on Earth.

3. **Hook (stat punch):** 92 antiprotons. ~1,000 kg cryogenic trap. Half-hour Meyrin loop. Vacuum < 2.2 × 10⁻¹⁸ mbar. More than a month of storage after the drive. Losses on the road: 0.

4. **Hook (stakes):** CERN’s Antimatter Factory is too magnetically noisy for the next decimal places of matter–antimatter tests. The fix is logistics: move the antiprotons to quiet labs — starting with a truck.

5. **Hook (identity / shareability):** Somewhere in Geneva, antimatter rode shotgun. Not as fuel. As cargo. Scientists just proved the universe’s rarest freight can survive a road trip.
