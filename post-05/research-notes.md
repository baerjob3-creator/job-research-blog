## Caveats for editors / Website Developer

1. **Not antimatter fuel / weapons / sci-fi cargo:** Always frame as a microscopic scientific sample in an ultrahigh-vacuum Penning trap. Do not imply energy applications.
2. **On-site dress rehearsal, not intercity delivery yet:** March 24, 2026 run stayed on CERN’s Meyrin site (~7.5–8 km). Geneva→Düsseldorf (~8–10 hours) is the *next* goal and still needs cryocooler/generator autonomy plus safe transfer at destination.

4. **Zero loss applies to the road transport:** One antiproton annihilated on day 27 during a deliberate extraction test — not during driving. Do not blur those events.
5. **Author roles:** Leonhardt = lead author; Smorra = BASE-STEP PI / corresponding; Ulmer = BASE spokesperson / coauthor. Do not collapse credits.
6. **Publication vs experiment dates:** Transport Mar 24, 2026; *Nature* VoR Sep 16, 2026. Do not write “discovered today” as if Sep 20 were the experiment day.
7. **Quotes:** Only use Ulmer / Smorra / Leonhardt / Hamel de Monchenault lines from CERN/HHU — do not fabricate.
8. **Prior art:** Proton transport (Oct 2024 / *Nature* 2025) proved the truck with matter; novelty is *antiprotons* without loss in an open mobile trap, plus record vacuum for that class.
